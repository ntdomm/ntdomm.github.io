Short Movie Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 Developer / legal entity: ntdogstudio Contact: ntdogdev@gmail.com

Short Movie ("the app", "we", "us") is a free, ad-supported app for watching short films and AI-assisted short clips. The app does not require an account.

Information we handle

  • Device-local preferences and activity: a random viewer ID, language, autoplay/data-saver/notification settings, likes, saves, follows, and locally stored comments (if comments are enabled).
  • Usage events: plays, completions, views, ad impressions, and basic app diagnostics sent to our API.
  • Advertising data: when ads are shown, Appodeal and its mediated ad networks may collect device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data, and consent signals.
  • Content delivery data: Mux processes video requests and may log IP address, device/browser information, and playback diagnostics to deliver streams.
  • Support communications: information you send to us when you contact support.

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information because the app has no login or in-app purchase flow.

How information is used

  • Operate the feed, catalog, search, likes, saves, and settings.
  • Deliver video and thumbnails through Mux.
  • Show and measure ads through Appodeal and its partners.
  • Measure basic usage, prevent abuse, and maintain security and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Local storage and retention

Likes, saves, follows, preferences, and any local comments are stored on your device using app storage. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this local data. Server-side events are retained only as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or aggregated.

Advertising and consent

Ads are provided by Appodeal and its mediated networks. Where required, a consent manager (such as Google UMP/TCF) asks for your choices before personalized ads are used. You can change or withdraw consent through the app's privacy options, if available, or by clearing app data. When consent is denied, ads should be non-personalized.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Because the app has no account, most data is device-local; clear app data or uninstall to remove it. For server-side requests, contact ntdogdev@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us.

AI-assisted content

Some clips may be created or edited with AI tools. We aim to label AI-assisted content and require creators to have rights to the material. AI labels are disclosed in the app where applicable.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted with a new effective date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
