Short Movie AI Content Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 Contact: ntdogdev@gmail.com

Short Movie may host short films and clips that are created or edited with generative AI tools. This policy explains how AI-assisted content is labeled, reviewed, and reported.

Disclosure

  • AI-assisted content should be labeled at upload and in the player where feasible.
  • Labels should be accurate and not misleading. A label does not imply endorsement or ownership.
  • Content that realistically depicts real people, events, or voices must not impersonate or deceive. Deepfakes and non-consensual intimate imagery are prohibited.

Rights and ownership

  • Creators must own or have permission for all input material, including images, video, music, voices, and likenesses.
  • AI output alone does not guarantee copyright protection. Creators are responsible for their claims and licenses.
  • Third-party trademarks, characters, and music must be cleared.

Review

We may review AI-assisted content for:

  • Sexual content, nudity, and CSAM.
  • Violence, self-harm, hate, harassment, and extremism.
  • Impersonation, fraud, and misleading synthetic media.
  • Copyright and likeness violations.
  • Misleading health, political, or financial claims.

We may remove, age-gate, or decline content that violates these standards.

Reporting

To report content, contact ntdogdev@gmail.com with the film URL/ID, a description of the issue, and supporting details. Reports involving imminent harm should also be directed to local authorities.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted with a new effective date.
